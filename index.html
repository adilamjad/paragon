Install Paragon on Your Phone

Use it like a real native app with offline support and zero browser bars:

  4. Tap "Add" in the top right. It will appear right on your home screen!
🤖 On Android (Chrome):
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in top right.
  3. Tap "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
